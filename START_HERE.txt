STREAM CLIPPER - 3 LAST STEPS
=============================

Setup is done: Python, the packages and the clipper agent are installed,
and the agent runs hidden in the background (it also starts with Windows).

1) ADD THE SKILL TO CLAUDE
   - Open the Claude desktop app -> Settings -> Skills (or Capabilities -> Skills)
   - Upload "stream-clipper-skill.zip" from this folder.

2) SIGN IN TO YOUTUBE
   - In the Claude app, open the built-in browser and go to https://studio.youtube.com
   - Sign in to the channel you want to post Shorts on.
     (Claude never types your password - you sign in yourself.)

3) RUN IT
   - In a new Claude chat, type:  /stream-clipper
   - First time: Claude asks "How many clips do you want per video?" -> pick a number.
     It's saved. Say "change my clip count" anytime.
   - When Claude asks for access to Videos\clipper, click Allow.
   - Choose "Clipping", paste a Twitch or YouTube stream URL, and let it work.
   - When a clip is ready, Claude opens the YouTube upload box: drag the clip in.

Tips
 - NVIDIA GPU? Double-click install_gpu.bat for faster transcription (about 1 GB).
 - Always credit the streamer. Reposting other people's streams can get copyright claims.
